Singaplores

Game Overview

Little Sheep: Singaplores is a cooperative tabletop game where 2–4 players guide a group of sheep tourists around Singapore. Over the course of three in-game festivals, players must explore the city, try different local foods, manage their tummy health, and make sure everyone survives the trip without getting a stomachache or running out of money.

Game Concept

  • Genre: Family-friendly cooperative strategy game
  • Players: 2–4
  • Core Fantasy: You are tourists navigating Singapore’s food and festival culture, balancing curiosity with survival.
  • Success: Work together to survive all three festivals and complete the trip.
  • Failure: If the group’s tummy health collapses or key lose conditions are met, everyone loses together.

Core Systems & Mechanics

  • Map Navigation: Players move their sheep around a stylised map of Singapore, visiting locations such as food centres, attractions, housing areas and toilets.
  • Resource Management:
    • Money: Used to buy food and travel. Overspending limits future options.
    • Tummy / Health: Eating risky food or drawing bad events can reduce tummy health and lead to diarrhea (immediate danger state).
    • Time / Days: A day counter tracks the length of the trip across three festivals.
  • Food Cards: Different dishes offer a mix of benefits and risks. Some restore energy or give small bonuses, others are “worth it but dangerous” and can trigger tummy problems.
  • Event Cards: Random events (e.g. transport disruptions, weather, crowds, special deals) force the group to adapt their route and priorities each day.
  • Travel System: Moving between areas often involves public transport. Certain routes or repeated MRT usage become more costly or risky, encouraging efficient path planning.
  • Festival Structure: The trip is divided into three festival phases, each escalating the pressure with tighter resources, stronger events, and more tempting food.

Turn & Round Flow (High Level)

  1. Day Setup: Update the day counter and resolve any start-of-day events.
  2. Planning: As a group, decide where to travel to next based on money, tummy health, nearby toilets, and which foods or locations you still want to visit.
  3. Movement & Actions: Move along the map, pay any travel costs, buy and share food, and resolve location effects.
  4. Events & Consequences: Draw and resolve event cards that can help or hinder the group.
  5. End of Day: Check for festival milestones and lose conditions; advance to the next day until all three festivals are completed or the group fails.

Design Goals

  • Accessible but Strategic: Simple core actions with emergent difficulty from map routing, event randomness, and shared resource decisions.
  • Cooperative Tension: Players must negotiate which foods to risk, when to detour to toilets, and how to split money so that no one sheep becomes a liability.
  • Local Flavour: Showcase Singapore’s culture, food and public transport in a playful, light-hearted way without overwhelming new players.

My Responsibilities

  • Designed the overall game concept and cooperative survival structure.
  • Built the map flow and travel rules to create meaningful route planning.
  • Authored and balanced food and event cards to control difficulty across the three festivals.
  • Wrote the rulebook and created printable components for physical prototyping and playtesting.
