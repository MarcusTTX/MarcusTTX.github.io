Virtual SIT

Virtual SIT is an interactive 1:1 digital recreation of the Singapore Institute of Technology’s new Punggol campus, built in Unity to help students, staff, and visitors explore the environment before stepping on-site.

The project provides an immersive way for:

  • New students to familiarise themselves with the new campus layout.
  • Professors and staff to understand facilities and plan events more effectively.
  • Faculty and planners to visualise room usage, movement flow, and venue arrangements.

Key Features

  • 1:1 scale digital campus
    A faithful recreation of the SIT Punggol campus, built room-by-room using the Unity game engine.
  • Multiple explorable scenes
    Includes different campus spaces such as lecture halls, staff rooms, labs, and teaching rooms to showcase their layout and function.
  • Interactable campus vehicle
    A drivable shuttle/vehicle that lets users travel between major areas, simulating real campus movement.
  • Guided exploration experience
    Designed to help first-time visitors, students, and staff navigate the new campus confidently.

Purpose & Impact

  • Acts as a visualisation tool for event planning and facility understanding.
  • Supports orientation for incoming students before they arrive on campus.
  • Serves as a showcase of the SIT Punggol campus prior to full physical access.
  • Provides a testbed for future virtual campus features and student learning tools.
