Ward_404

Game Concept

A narrative puzzle-based game that relies on the player’s environment and surroundings to paint a story for the player to experience. The player starts off in the reception area of a hospital and tries to figure out the reason they are in this area. As the player slowly traverses the different levels, they find themself playing as a “doctor” in a hospital environment, trying to figure out the objective. At the end of each level, the player questions themself further as the environment gets weirder, setting up the final reveal at the end of the game.

Unique Points & Mechanics

As a game that leans more towards the narrative and the story-telling concept, it is heavily reliant on non-player characters (NPCs) to set up the scenario and environment for the player. The NPCs have their own individual animations and set paths so as to paint a realistic scene for the player in the level. The game level also features highly realistic environments where the player can interact with certain objects to clear their current objective. The player is directed to their objective through the usage of voicelines of the NPCs as they progress through the hospital area.
For example, as the player progresses to level 2, they get informed by a nurse at the reception that a patient is in distress and should proceed there immediately.
Once the player reaches the patient’s bedside, the player realises that the patient is currently having an allergic reaction and requires medication. They will then proceed to the medicine room located in level 2 where it is locked and the player has to search for the key. After the player manages to find the key, they can then proceed to the medicine room in order to find the medicine for the sick patient in ward 202. Once the player enters the medicine room, they will then find different types of medicine for treating specific illnesses and a book. By interacting with the book, it would inform the player of what type of medicine is suitable for which illness so that the player can bring the correct medicine to the patient.
Note this project is only made for alpha stage
